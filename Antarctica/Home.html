Welcome to Antarctica 🧊

Antarctica is the coldest, windiest, and driest continent on Earth. No one lives here forever - only scientists, penguins, seals, and whales. It is a place of pure ice, silence, and beauty.

Did You Know?
Antarctica holds about 70% of the world's fresh water, locked in ice. If all of it melted, sea levels would rise by about 60 meters!

How To Get In ✈️🚢

There are no airports for tourists and no roads to Antarctica. You can only go by expedition ship or by special flight.

🚢 BY SHIP

From: Ushuaia, Argentina

Time: 2 days across Drake Passage

The most popular way. You sail through the roughest sea in the world to reach the White Continent.

✈️ BY PLANE

From: Punta Arenas, Chile

Time: 2 hours flight

Faster but more expensive. You land on a blue ice runway and then explore by ship.
